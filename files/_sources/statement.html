GDPR Compliance Statement

Version 1.0 · Issued May 26, 2026 · Next review November 2026

This statement records Voight's alignment with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the national legislation that implements it.

Voight is incorporated in the United States with a main establishment in Spain (Article 4(16)). The Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) is the competent supervisory authority. An Article 27 representative is not required.

Processing relies on a single lawful basis: performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b). Voight does not profile users, run behavioural advertising, or sell personal data.

International transfers are governed by the European Commission's 2021 Standard Contractual Clauses, supported by encryption at rest and in transit, pseudonymisation by design, and least-privilege access.

Three sub-processors deliver the service — Vercel (hosting), Railway (database), and Privy (authentication) — each SOC 2 Type II certified with a published GDPR DPA.

Data subject rights under Articles 12–22 are honoured within thirty days. Privacy-by-design filters run locally before any telemetry leaves the customer's process. Personal data is retained on a tiered schedule and deleted on request, including from backups within the next rotation cycle.
