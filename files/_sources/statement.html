GDPR Compliance Statement

Version 1.0 · Issued May 26, 2026 · Next review November 2026

Voight publishes this statement to record, on the public record, its alignment with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — the General Data Protection Regulation — and the national legislation that implements it.

Voight is incorporated in the United States with a main establishment in Spain, where the founding team takes and implements decisions concerning the processing of personal data. For the purposes of Article 4(16) of the Regulation, the Spanish establishment is Voight's main establishment in the Union, and the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) is the competent supervisory authority. Voight is therefore not required to designate a representative under Article 27.

Voight relies on a single lawful basis for the processing it controls — performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b). Voight does not profile users, does not run behavioural advertising, and does not sell or share personal data with third parties for such purposes. International transfers of personal data are governed by the European Commission's 2021 Standard Contractual Clauses and are supported by encryption in transit and at rest, pseudonymisation by design, and the supplementary measures set out in the full documentation.

Three sub-processors deliver the service today: Vercel (hosting), Railway (database and API), and Privy (authentication). Each has signed or published a GDPR Data Processing Agreement and holds a current SOC 2 Type II report. The complete register, including planned sub-processors, is maintained in the documentation referenced below.

Data subject rights under Articles 12 to 22 are honoured within the statutory thirty-day window. Privacy-by-design controls run locally in the customer's own process before any telemetry leaves it. Personal data is retained on a tiered schedule and deleted on request, including from backups within the next rotation cycle.
